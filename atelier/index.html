03 / How the work takes shape

The details connect.
So should the brief.

A fixture has to show the product, fit its setting and be assembled. We discuss those conditions together before defining the next step.

  1. 01

    Define the use

    Product dimensions, display location, customer access and installation constraints.

  2. 02

    Resolve the construction

    Discuss metal, wood and acrylic integration, drawings and sample requirements.

  3. 03

    Plan the handover

    Agree the project scope for production follow-up, quality checks, packing and export coordination.
