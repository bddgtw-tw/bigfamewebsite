Project routes / Brand · Design · Procurement

Start with what
you know.

An early idea and a finished drawing need different conversations. Choose the starting point closest to your project.

01

A display that fits
your product.

For brand teams developing a retail presentation.

Start a brand brief →

Bring the retail context

  • The product to display, including size and how shoppers handle it.
  • The setting: countertop, wall or freestanding fixture.
  • Your estimated quantity, destination and any installation constraints.

We can discuss fixture formats, display hardware and materials before defining what needs a drawing or sample.

02

A concept with
construction in mind.

For retail designers developing a fixture or system.

Start a design brief →

Bring the design intent

  • Overall dimensions and the parts of the design that must stay fixed.
  • Preferred metal, wood, acrylic or mixed-material direction.
  • Mounting, assembly and finish questions that remain open.

We can help clarify construction questions and sample requirements. Share drawings later through an agreed channel; this preview accepts no uploads.

03

A sourcing request
with fewer gaps.

For procurement teams preparing a project enquiry.

Start a procurement brief →

Bring the buying conditions

  • The product type and drawing or specification status.
  • Estimated quantities and destination markets.
  • Packaging, labeling and inspection requirements to discuss.

We can review the scope for production follow-up and export coordination. Pricing, minimum quantities and timing are confirmed for each project.
