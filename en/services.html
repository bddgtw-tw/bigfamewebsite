BRAND BESPOKE & PROTOTYPING

5 Agile Stages of Custom Store Fixture Development

Overcoming traditional factory barriers of rigid high-MOQ demands, Big Fame provides an agile manufacturing pathway from initial prototype to volume production:

01. Concept Scoping & 3D Engineering

Bring hand sketches, mood boards, or product dimensions. We translate design ideas into 3D CAD fabrication models with Value Engineering (VE).

02. 1:1 Low MOQ Prototyping

Produce 1:1 functional samples for brand review, verifying load capacity, joint tolerances, powder coat finishes, and merchandising ergonomics.

03. Mixed-Material Integration

Single-window accountability across steel fabrication, powder coating, joinery, acrylic crafting, and custom display hardware.

04. Small-Batch Pop-Up Runs (10–50 Sets)

Leveraging modular core frameworks with custom exterior branding accents, achieving tailored aesthetics with controlled budgets and quick lead times.

05. Flat-Pack Packaging & Global Delivery

Engineered for rapid on-site assembly with protective flat-pack export crating, supporting regional store delivery or international freight.

01

Requirement Scoping & Value Engineering

We collaborate with client design and procurement teams to clarify load capacities, paint gloss, and chemical resistance. Our Taipei team offers "Value Engineering (VE)" advice on tooling and hardware structures, lowering production costs without sacrificing strength or brand aesthetics.

02

Design Engineering & Prototyping

Based on finalized blueprints, our skilled craftspeople manufacture high-precision physical samples. This allows clients to test assembly tolerances, weld finishes, and powder coating texture, making micro-adjustments before mass production.

03

Manufacturing Integration & Progress Tracking

Products are routed to specialized facilities based on fabrication process (extrusion, stamping, die-casting). Taipei PMs control progress across all component plants to ensure on-time assembly aggregation.

04

Secondary Quality Control at Logistical Warehouses

Where required by the project, manufactured batches can be consolidated through a designated logistics point for secondary checks such as trial assembly, appearance, quantity and packing confirmation. Inspection scope is agreed against the approved specification.

05

Industrial Packing & Multilingual Instructions

To withstand long ocean shipping journeys, we use reinforced boxes and edge protectors. We coordinate cross-factory consolidation, manage customs clearance, and collaborate with global freight forwarders to deliver to your stores or warehouses.

06

Consolidation & Global DDP Logistics

We arrange factory-wide consolidation, container packing coordinates, export customs declaration, and delivery to designated ports or warehouses globally.
