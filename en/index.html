FAQ

FAQ

What display equipment can Big Fame support?

Start with display hooks, modular fixtures, POS displays, slatwall or pegboard accessories, price-tag holders and custom metal parts; formal specifications and delivery scope are confirmed by project.

Can we start with a photo or CAD file?

Yes. Photos, PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP, merchandise dimensions, quantity, target timing and destination can start the discussion.

Does every product have the same MOQ and lead time?

No universal MOQ or lead time applies to every product. Confirm by model, material, quantity, sample, revision and schedule.

Have a retail or display hardware requirement?

You do not need the exact SKU first. Share a photo, drawing or the problem you are facing and start from the right entry.
