BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

Common Project Questions

Can I start with sketches or reference photos?

Yes. Share your product requirements, a drawing or photo link, estimated quantity and destination. We will review the scope and clarify what information is needed next.

Can I request a sample or a small production run?

Discuss your required quantity and sample purpose with us. Feasibility, minimum quantities, cost and timing depend on the design, materials and production process and are confirmed by quotation.

How do you approach packaging and international shipping?

We review assembly, packaging and shipping requirements with the project. Knock-down construction may be an option; dimensions, packing method, origin and freight arrangements are confirmed for the specific order.
