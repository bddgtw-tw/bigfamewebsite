VELTOR — Heavy-Load KD Flagship Retail System Study

Space Type
Specialty Moto & Touring Flagship (Concept)
Classification
Concept Study
Manufacturing Scope
Heavy-duty wall shelving, KD steel-and-oak island tables, garment racks, reception counter
Primary Materials
Powder-coated matte steel, brushed oak veneer, olive laminate cabinetry, integrated LED

Design & Engineering Challenge

Developed as an architectural fixture system study for outdoor and motorcycle gear retail, demonstrating how heavy merchandise (helmets, leather boots, and touring luggage) can sit on refined, repeatable KD modular structures.

Joinery, Structure & Delivery Execution

  • KD Central Island Table: Bolted dark-steel perimeter frame supporting two-tier brushed oak decks — designed to pack flat for ocean freight and assemble rapidly on site without welding.
  • Heavy-Load Wall Bays: Matte steel upright frames integrated with concealed LED shelf channels, oak shelving, and olive-toned lower stock drawers.
  • Unified Storewide Material Palette: Garment racks and wood-clad reception counters share identical finish codes to keep multi-store expansions visually coherent.
Specifications & Delivery Notes: Panel dimensions, finish sheen, structural load ratings, and KD export crating specifications are confirmed per project drawings and site conditions.
