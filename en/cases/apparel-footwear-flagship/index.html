Apparel & Footwear Flagship — Modular Steel Fixture System

Space Type
Apparel & Footwear Flagship Retail
Classification
Completed Installation
Manufacturing Scope
Heavy-duty garment racks, nesting display tables, cantilevered wall shoe shelves, perimeter shelving
Primary Materials
Matte powder-coated square steel tubing, laminated tabletops, concealed wall brackets

Design & Engineering Challenge

A multi-category apparel and footwear flagship required slim, understated steel fixtures strong enough to carry heavy outerwear and dense footwear walls without visual clutter, while allowing rapid floor reconfiguration across seasons.

Joinery, Structure & Delivery Execution

  • Anti-Sag Garment & Nesting Frames: Precision-welded square steel tubing with uniform matte powder coating, supporting heavy coats and layered folding displays cleanly.
  • Cantilevered Footwear Wall: Concealed wall-mounted brackets supporting crisp horizontal shoe shelves along the perimeter bay.
  • Modular Floor Rollout: Standardized table heights and rack footprints designed for flexible seasonal VMD layouts and repeatable multi-store production.
Specifications & Delivery Notes: Panel dimensions, finish sheen, structural load ratings, and KD export crating specifications are confirmed per project drawings and site conditions.
