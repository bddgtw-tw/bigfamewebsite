Japanese Lounge & Wine Bar — Backlit Millwork & Stone Counter

Space Type
Hospitality Lounge & Wine Bar
Classification
Completed Installation
Manufacturing Scope
Backlit recessed display wall, tempered glass shelving, concealed lower cabinetry, stone bar counter
Primary Materials
Lacquered architectural millwork, recessed linear LED, clear tempered glass, polished dark stone

Design & Engineering Challenge

For an intimate Japanese-style hospitality bar, the designer sought a glowing architectural bottle gallery where concealed vertical lighting, razor-thin glass shelves, and dark reflective stone work together without visible wiring or bulky brackets.

Joinery, Structure & Delivery Execution

  • Concealed Linear LED Joinery: Milled flush light channels directly into the vertical millwork dividers so illumination washes evenly across every bay.
  • Tempered Glass Shelf Alignment: Precision-drilled concealed shelf supports holding clear glass tiers level across adjacent recessed bays.
  • Stone Bar & Storage Integration: Coordinated dark stone bar top with handle-less lower service cabinetry for seamless front-of-house elegance and back-of-house durability.
Specifications & Delivery Notes: Panel dimensions, finish sheen, structural load ratings, and KD export crating specifications are confirmed per project drawings and site conditions.
