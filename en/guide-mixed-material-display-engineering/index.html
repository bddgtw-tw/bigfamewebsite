Plan a display in metal, wood and acrylic

A display should look right, fit together and work in its store setting. Discuss those needs before selecting the construction.

Start with use and appearance

Describe what the display holds, how customers use it, and which surfaces they see and touch. Product weight, cleaning and installation conditions help guide material choices.

Review the connections

Where different materials meet, agree the fit, finish, fastening and assembly details. Drawings and samples can help resolve those questions; tolerances need to be set for the particular design.

Plan the packing

Discuss whether the display should ship assembled or in separate parts, what protection it needs, and who will assemble it. Shipping volume and costs must be checked against the actual design and packing plan.

Agree the checks

Big Fame can coordinate drawing and sample review, supplier follow-up and agreed quality checks. Specify any load testing, material certificates or destination requirements before production.

Start with what you have.

Tell us about the product, display space, estimated quantity, destination and target timing. Open questions are welcome.
