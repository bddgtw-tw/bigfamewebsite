FAQ

FAQ

Is this a completed retail-store installation case?

It is published as an anonymous procurement and document-integration record. The sources support the order list, PIs, categories, units and delivery fields; the public page does not claim a named installation, formal quantity or final site result.

Can Big Fame coordinate several fixture categories in one purchase?

Start with the store type, plan or photos, category list, product dimensions, quantities, target schedule and delivery location. We can then separate fixtures, hooks, acrylic accessories, tables and signage into a confirmation path.

What should I prepare before sourcing?

Share the store zones, displayed products, dimensions, quantities, material direction, packing needs, target date, delivery location and PDF, CAD or photos. Formal MOQ, lead time and quotation terms are confirmed item by item.
