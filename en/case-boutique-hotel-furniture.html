Anonymous hospitality / furniture coordination

Coordinate the furniture across your guestrooms.

The published project record describes a guestroom furniture collection and the coordination of materials and form. It does not establish a complete manufacturing, installation or delivery contract.

What the record covers

Wardrobes, minibars, writing desks, side tables, headboards and bed frames.

A shared material direction

The record describes expanded metal mesh combined with wood, pipe, lighting and pattern across the furniture collection.

Confirm for your project

Agree the furniture list, drawings, sample scope, finishes and delivery responsibilities. Historical room counts are not confirmed purchase quantities.

What to prepare

Bring a room plan, furniture list, approximate dimensions, room or item quantities, material direction and destination.
