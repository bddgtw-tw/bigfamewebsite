ENGINEERING & MANUFACTURING

Cross-Material Fabrication, KD Packaging & Regional Supply

Instead of splitting metal frames, wood cabinetry, acrylic trays, and stone countertops across disconnected vendors, Big Fame coordinates assembly tolerances and export logistics under one roof.

01

Multi-Material Retail Fixtures (OEM / ODM)

Modular wall systems, heavy-duty gondolas, island tables, and specialty showcases combining powder-coated steel, bent tubing, birch plywood, solid wood, acrylic, and tempered glass.

  • DFM review of metal-to-wood, glass, and stone interfaces prior to tooling
  • Revision-controlled drawing numbering for multi-year store rollouts
  • Dimensions, structural load ratings, and finishes confirmed per project drawings
02

Knock-Down (KD) Flat-Pack & TCO Engineering

Moving beyond unit FOB pricing to Total Cost of Ownership — designing bolted, flat-pack breakdown structures from prototyping to reduce ocean freight volume and on-site installation friction.

  • Flat-pack structural breakdown evaluated against container and pallet dimensions
  • Factory 1:1 pre-shipment dry-fit inspection and labeled hardware kits
  • Supported by Taiwan warehousing and regional export consolidation
03

Hospitality FF&E, Vietnam Stone & Regional Hubs

Custom guestroom casegoods, bent-metal wardrobes, outdoor hospitality seating, and natural granite or marble countertops processed at our Vietnam stone facility.

  • Direct Vietnam granite & marble processing integrated with metal frames and cabinetry
  • Malaysia regional hospitality & outdoor FF&E support via Sunnyward Sdn Bhd
  • Strict white-label OEM ethics and NDA protection for design studios and trading agents
All engineering tolerances, container CBM calculations, material specifications, surface treatments, and lead times are confirmed per project drawings, order volume, and destination requirements.
