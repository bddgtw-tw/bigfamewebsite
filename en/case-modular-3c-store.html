ANONYMOUS PROJECT / 3C RETAIL

Second-hand 3C Retail Modular Display System

A Taipei first-store brief covering high-value electronics display, repeatability, shipping and assembly.

PROJECT EVIDENCE

Client problem

The client wanted a distinctive second-hand 3C store for MacBook Air, iPhone, iPad, iMac, cameras and game consoles, with a display system that could be duplicated in other cities.

What was developed?

The project record describes a modular direction: rectangular pipe and horizontal connectors for wall display, add-on glass showcases, and shared cabinets with replaceable tops for different functions. Easy shipping and assembly were part of the brief.
