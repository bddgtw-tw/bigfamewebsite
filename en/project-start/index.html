Project routes / Brand · Design · Procurement

Start with what
you know.

An early idea and a finished drawing need different conversations. Choose the starting point closest to your project.

Prepare only what you already have. Describe the references you have; agree a suitable channel for sharing files. A first reply is for clarifying the project, with no promised quotation date or outcome.

01

A display that fits
your product.

For brand teams developing a retail presentation.

Discuss a brand project →

Bring the retail context

  • Product dimensions or a description of available product photos, and how shoppers handle the item.
  • The setting: countertop, wall or freestanding fixture. Note whether a space plan or site dimensions are available.
  • Estimated quantity, destination and target launch or installation timing, including what is still flexible.

The next conversation

After you send your brief, our reply can help clarify the display format, missing dimensions and which drawing or sample would help assess the idea. Scope and feasibility need review before a quotation or production plan.

02

A concept with
construction in mind.

For retail designers developing a fixture or system.

Discuss a design project →

Bring the design intent

  • Product dimensions or photo references, a space plan or fixture sketch, and the design details that must stay fixed.
  • Preferred metal, wood, acrylic or mixed-material direction.
  • Mounting, assembly and finish questions, plus estimated quantity, destination and target timing.

The next conversation

Our reply can identify construction questions, how the different materials will fit together, and drawing or sample steps to discuss. The scope of design support and any production work is agreed for your project.

03

A sourcing request
with fewer gaps.

For procurement teams preparing a project enquiry.

Discuss procurement →

Bring the buying conditions

  • Product dimensions or photo references and the current drawing/specification status. Note any space or installation plan available.
  • Estimated quantities, destination markets and target delivery timing, including any fixed launch date.
  • Packaging, labeling and inspection requirements to discuss.

The next conversation

Our reply can identify specification gaps and the information needed to discuss sampling, production follow-up, quality checks and export coordination. Pricing, minimum quantities and timing require project-specific confirmation.
