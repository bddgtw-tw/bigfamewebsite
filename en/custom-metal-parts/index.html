SPECIFICATION CHECKLIST

What should be confirmed first?

Drawings and samples

Start with photos, PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP, concept sketches or existing sample information for a traceable brief.

Material and dimensions

Confirm metal type or grade, wire/tube/sheet thickness, overall dimensions, key tolerances and assembly method.

Finish and use conditions

Describe finish, color, load, environment, packaging and transport constraints instead of judging from an image alone.

Quantity and timing

Share estimated quantity, first-batch need, replenishment pattern and target launch date. MOQ and lead time require case-by-case review.

Who is this for?

Useful for buyers, retail planners, architects, visual merchandisers and display accessory owners preparing an inquiry.

FAQ

Custom inquiry FAQ

What can I provide to start a review?

Photos, PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP, concept sketches or existing sample information. Feasibility still depends on the actual brief.

Is there a universal MOQ or lead time?

No. MOQ and lead time depend on the part, material, process, quantity and packaging requirements.

SPECIFICATION GATE

Specification fields to confirm before sourcing

These fields form the shared confirmation frame before a formal quotation. A field becomes a formal specification only when it is supported by the matching SKU, drawing, quotation or sample.

Store format and system

Confirm the store type, backing or mounting system, displayed product and site conditions.

Material and finish

Confirm material grade, colour, finish and appearance against the SKU, drawing, quotation and sample.

Dimensions and load conditions

Confirm dimensions, wire diameter, board thickness, load or fixing method against the representative drawing and project conditions.

MOQ and lead time

There is no universal MOQ or lead time for every product; confirm by type, quantity, material, finish and project.

Customization scope

Share dimensions, photos, drawings, quantity and use conditions so changes to size, material, finish or structure can be assessed.

Evidence and formal files

Representative images, drawings and cases support initial evaluation; formal PDF, CAD, samples and commercial terms follow the approved version.
