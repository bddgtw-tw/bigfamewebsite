What the existing records actually show

These are product and development records, not a promise of current stock or a completed multi-store rollout.

YC-1524L

Mobile display fixture

YC-1524L records describe a metal slot fixture in two widths: 24 or 48 inches, both 30 inches deep and 56 inches high, with casters. Useful as a starting point when footprint and mobility matter.

ARC67-A

Freestanding panel display

ARC67-A records describe a sock rack with four white acrylic panels and casters. Its documented footprint is 24.5 × 24.5 inches. Confirm the merchandise and access requirements before choosing this direction.

IVY

A coordinated fixture system

IVY development and sample records cover an island set, uprights, frames, covers and base connections. They support discussion of repeatable components; they do not establish a delivered store rollout.

Model, current availability, materials, load requirements, packing, quantities and timing must be confirmed for the project.

Decide what stays common—and what changes.

Product & space

Share product dimensions, available width/depth/height, and a reference photo or layout. Identify what differs between stores.

Assembly & packing

Tell us who will assemble the display, where it will be unpacked and whether it needs to move. Packing volume and assembly effort require an actual design review.

Sample & next step

We can discuss the missing dimensions, material and finish options, a suitable drawing/sample review, and the scope needed for a quotation. Timing and price depend on that review.

Can I start without a finished drawing?

Yes. A reference image, approximate dimensions, estimated quantity and delivery location are enough to begin a discussion. The next step is to clarify what remains unknown, not to assume the design is production-ready.
