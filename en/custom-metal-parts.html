SPECIFICATION CHECKLIST

What should be confirmed first?

Drawings and samples

Start with photos, PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP, concept sketches or existing sample information for a traceable brief.

Material and dimensions

Confirm metal type or grade, wire/tube/sheet thickness, overall dimensions, key tolerances and assembly method.

Finish and use conditions

Describe finish, color, load, environment, packaging and transport constraints instead of judging from an image alone.

Quantity and timing

Share estimated quantity, first-batch need, replenishment pattern and target launch date. MOQ and lead time require case-by-case review.

Evidence boundary

The image on this page is illustrative. It does not represent a specific SKU, dimension, material or load rating. Confirmed drawings and quotation documents control final specifications.

Who is this for?

Useful for buyers, retail planners, architects, visual merchandisers and display accessory owners preparing an inquiry.
