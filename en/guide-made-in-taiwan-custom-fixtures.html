Plan your Taiwan sourcing enquiry

Bring the display requirement and buying conditions together so the next discussion can focus on your project.

Choose a starting point

Start with a product category, a sketch or an existing drawing. Explain what must stay fixed and where alternatives can be considered.

Compare the whole requirement

Discuss estimated quantities, materials, finish, packing, destination and target timing together. Minimum quantities and sample arrangements vary by project.

Understand Big Fame’s role

Big Fame can help clarify the brief and coordinate manufacturing partners, samples, production follow-up, quality checks and export work. The agreed scope determines each party’s responsibilities.

Confirm origin and delivery terms

Ask for the origin, shipping terms and documents needed for the particular goods and destination. A Taiwan-based contact is not a blanket guarantee that every item is made in Taiwan.

Start with what you have.

Tell us about the product, display space, estimated quantity, destination and target timing. Open questions are welcome.
