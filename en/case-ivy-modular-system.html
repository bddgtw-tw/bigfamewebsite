IVY / system development record

Plan a display system around your space.

IVY records show how wall and island fixtures can be developed as a coordinated set of components. This is development and sample-drawing evidence, not a completed store rollout.

What was developed

The record includes an island set, uprights, frames, top covers and base connections.

Why the details matter

These components are a starting point for discussing configuration, assembly and how the parts work together. The current approved drawing must govern any new production.

Confirm for your project

Discuss the configuration and sample scope first. Materials, quantities, packing and timing remain subject to review.

What to prepare

Bring a floor plan or sketch, merchandise dimensions, wall or island requirements, estimated quantity and destination.
