How this preview handles your brief

The brief checker keeps your entries in the current page. It does not submit them to Big Fame, persist them in browser storage, or send analytics events. Downloading a summary creates a file on your device.

The email link opens your own email application with your summary. Nothing is sent until you choose to send. Your email provider will process that message under its terms. Only share drawings you are authorized to share, and do not include passwords, private customer data or restricted documents.

Online form delivery and analytics are not enabled in this preview. This notice will be reviewed before they are activated.
