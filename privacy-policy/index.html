Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an engineering inquiry on bigfame.co, we collect your company name, contact name, business email address, and any project specifications you voluntarily share so our team can respond to your request.

2. Analytics & Cookies (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-PDW4NPHHW8) to understand aggregate website traffic, language preferences, and inquiry conversion events (such as contact clicks and form submissions). We do not sell personal data to third parties.
